Takeaways

These findings suggest that age, gender, and occupation play a role in stress levels, which, in turn, indeed affect sleep and blood pressure. However, it is interesting to note that these factors don’t necessarily have the highest correlation with each other. Other factors, such as age and overall sleep, gender and stress, and occupation and heart rate, are more closely related than previously thought
